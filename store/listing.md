# Chrome Web Store listing

**Name:** Collection Matcher for Riftbound  **Short name:** RB Matcher

**Category:** Tools  **Language:** English

**Short description (≤132 chars):**
See how many copies of each Riftbound card you own, right next to card names on Piltover Archive, riftbound.gg and riftdecks.com.

**Full description:**

Collection Matcher for Riftbound shows your Riftbound collection while you browse decks.

• Deck pages show a small badge next to each card: owned/needed.
  – green: you own enough copies
  – amber: you own some
  – red: you own none
• Card galleries show how many copies you own (×N).
• Alt arts, overnumbered, signed, promo and foil printings all count toward the same card.
• Works on Piltover Archive, riftbound.gg and riftdecks.com. You can enable more sites one by one.
• Nothing else on the page changes. Hover previews, links and layout stay exactly as they were.

Get your collection in:
• Import a CSV export from riftbound.gg, Piltover Archive, RiftMana, RiftCore, OpenRift or CardNexus. You can also import any CSV with card and quantity columns, or a plain list like "3 Jinx, Rebel".
• Or keep it in sync automatically with your riftbound.gg "Permanent export link".

Private by design: no account, no server, no tracking. Your collection stays in your browser. You can export it as a backup file.

Card data comes from the public Riftcodex API.
